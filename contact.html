Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers regarding contractor accounts, orders, warranties, and bulk shipping.

How do I set up a commercial contractor account for bulk discounts?
You can apply online via our Pricing page or visit our Contractor Desk (Ext. 103) with your business license. Approved contractors receive tiered volume discounts, net-30 payment terms, and dedicated project manager assignment.
Are custom mixed paint formulas guaranteed for color matching accuracy?
Yes! Our digital spectrophotometer color-matching technology guarantees precision. If your custom blend does not match your physical swatch under natural lighting conditions, we will re-blend or refund your paint order free of charge.
What are your guidelines for heavy building material and pallet deliveries?
Pallet and heavy building supplies are delivered via our flatbed fleet equipped with hydraulic liftgates. Jobsite drop-off requests must specify accessibility for heavy commercial vehicles. Same-day delivery is available for orders confirmed before 12:00 PM.
What warranty options are included with industrial power tool purchases?
All professional-grade power tools carry manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 5 years. Additionally, Tool Hub offers an optional extended replacement warranty plan covering accidental jobsite damage and routine calibration maintenance.
