Company Background

Rooted in Quality & Hard Work

How we started and where we are heading

Founded over two decades ago as a modest hardware counter, Tool Hub started with a single mission: to provide trade professionals and homeowners access to industrial-grade tools and superior interior-exterior paint selections that stand the test of time.

Today, we have grown into a premier regional showroom offering computerized color-matching studios, expert maintenance workshops, and a dedicated commercial fulfillment fleet.

A hardware and paint store is a retail business that supplies products for construction, repair, maintenance, renovation, and painting projects.
